# HawkView — Tenant Formation

The mark depicts one forward-flying hawk assembled from three nested flight paths. The paths represent multiple managed tenants moving into one field of view. Its production geometry is traced from the approved No. 15 reference: a long upper wing, signal-blue center ribbon, swept lower wing, open white channels, and compact forward head.

## Palette

- Hawk Navy: `#022855`
- Signal Blue: `#0666EF`
- On-dark Signal Blue: `#2F7BFF`
- Interface Night: `#020B1A`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

- Preferred brand face: Manrope 700
- Product/UI companion: Inter 400–600
- Technical data: IBM Plex Mono 400–500

## Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the blue center ribbon (approximately 18% of the mark width) on every side. Do not place text, borders, or other symbols inside this area.

## Minimum size

- Full-color standalone mark: 32 px wide minimum
- Horizontal lockup: 140 px wide minimum
- Simplified app/favicon mark: 16 px minimum
- At 16–31 px, use `hawkview-favicon.svg`; it keeps the approved silhouette and adapts the navy paths for dark browser chrome.

## Usage

- Use the light lockup on white or very light neutral backgrounds.
- Use the dark lockup treatment on HawkView's slate sidebar and authentication surfaces.
- Use `hawkview-mark-on-dark.svg` when the mark appears without a container on a dark interface.
- Use the monochrome mark for consent screens, embossing, single-color printing, and constrained integrations.
- Do not rotate, outline, add code-rain effects to the core logo, change individual path colors, or separate the nested paths.

The detailed code-rain hawk remains a supporting campaign illustration. It should not replace this core mark at small sizes.
